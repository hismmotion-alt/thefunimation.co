Scouter — App Motion & Illustration

A football-led product story, brought to life through app screens, custom illustration, and character animation.

Challenge

Connect Scouter’s product screens with the energy of football in a short, cohesive visual story.

Approach

A vivid green palette, custom characters, kinetic typography, and animated interface sequences connect the app with its illustrated football world.

Result

A 27-second product film, supported by finished illustration frames and a storyboard showing the progression from initial sketches to final motion.
